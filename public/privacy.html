Privacy

LaunchFinder does not require an account to generate business-name ideas. When you use the site, the hosting platform may process ordinary technical information such as IP address, browser information, request timing, and server logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Business ideas

Text submitted to the generator is processed by LaunchFinder. When AI generation is enabled, that text may be sent to the configured AI provider to produce results. Do not submit secrets, passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive data.

Outbound links

LaunchFinder logs basic outbound-click events so we can understand which recommendations are used. Third-party websites you open have their own privacy policies and practices.

Local browser features

The site may use standard browser capabilities such as the clipboard when you choose to copy a generated name.
