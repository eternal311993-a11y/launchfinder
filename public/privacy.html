Privacy

LaunchFinder does not require an account to generate free business-name ideas. When you use the site, the hosting platform may process ordinary technical information such as IP address, browser information, request timing, and server logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Business ideas and AI generation

Text submitted to the generator is processed by LaunchFinder. When AI generation is enabled, that text may be sent to the configured AI provider to produce free results or a paid Launch Pack. Do not submit secrets, passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive data in business-description fields.

Payments

Paid Launch Pack checkout is processed by Stripe. Payment-card details are entered on Stripe's checkout experience and are not handled by LaunchFinder's application server. Stripe may process payment and transaction information under its own privacy terms.

Outbound links

LaunchFinder logs basic outbound-click events so we can understand which recommendations are used. Third-party websites you open have their own privacy policies and practices.

Local browser features

The site may use standard browser capabilities such as the clipboard when you choose to copy a generated name or Launch Pack.
