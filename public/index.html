FAQ

Before you choose a name

Does LaunchFinder guarantee the domain is available?

No. The domain text is a suggestion. Availability and pricing can change, so the registrar is the source of truth.

Does a generated name mean I can legally use it?

No. Generated names can still conflict with trademarks or existing businesses. Search relevant business and trademark records before committing.

How do I get better name ideas?

Include your target customer, location if it matters, specialty, desired tone, or what makes the business different.

Is it free?

LaunchFinder is free to try. Some outbound recommendations may use affiliate links, which can support the site without changing your purchase price.
